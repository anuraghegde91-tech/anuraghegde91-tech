# Hi, I'm Anurag Hegde

I'm a chemical engineer with 10 years in process safety, and I'm now moving into AI product management. For most of my career my job was to say go or no-go on chemical products and processes before they were scaled up. I worked with R&D, pilot plant, engineering and commercial teams, and a lot of it was explaining thermal hazard data in a way that made sense to them. I want to keep doing that kind of work, but on AI products.

Today, I bring that same approach to product thinking: understanding the problem, connecting user needs with business priorities, defining what to build and making thoughtful decisions about what to change or stop.

I also enjoy getting hands-on with AI, using modern development tools and AI coding agents to turn product ideas into working prototypes.

## How I Think About Products

- Start with the problem, not the solution.
- Understand who experiences the problem and why it matters.
- Separate assumptions from evidence.
- Build the simplest useful version.
- Define what success looks like before building.
- Know what to build, what to change and what to stop.

I believe good Product Management is as much about making the right decisions as it is about getting things built.
